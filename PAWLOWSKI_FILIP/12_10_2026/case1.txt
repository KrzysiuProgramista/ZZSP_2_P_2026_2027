  The queue empties after 52 search steps with visited = 52. The goal
  (10,9) IS in self.previous (it was discovered and enqueued), so the maze
  is reachable, but the program still reports failure.

  In faulty_solve at the line "if current == (self.goal[0], self.goal[1] - 1)".
  Reason: the BFS neighbour expansion looks correct (visited keeps growing
  normally), so the failure must be in the termination test. I watched
  `current` and `self.goal` when `current` became (10,9).

  continues until the queue is empty. The target (10,8) can never be
  dequeued because walls are never enqueued.

  with a shifted cell (goal column - 1). Trace evidence:
