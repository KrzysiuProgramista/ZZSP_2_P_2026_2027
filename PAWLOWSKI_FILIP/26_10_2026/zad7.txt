SYMPTOM: One of the expired sessions remains in the returned list.
CAUSE: Modifying (removing elements from) a list while iterating over it in a for loop causes skipped elements.
FIX: Created a new list containing only non-expired sessions using list comprehension instead of modifying the original list in place.
